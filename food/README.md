# Chili Night 2026

Real food, made from scratch. Happy Halloween!

## The menu

**1. Pick your chili**

- **Smoky Chili** (mild-medium): smoked pork shoulder, smoky pork belly bites, beef and
  pork, pinto and kidney beans, a whole-chile sauce.
- **Mild Veggie Chili** (mild, vegan): black and pinto beans, roasted sweet potato, corn
  and mild chiles. No meat, no dairy.

**2. Pick how you want it**

| | What it is | Smoky | Veggie |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chili Cup | A hot cup of chili, straight up (5 fl oz) | 180 cal | 120 cal |
| Chili & Chips | Crunchy corn chips under your choice of chili | 300 cal | 260 cal |
| Chili Mac | Elbow macaroni topped with your choice of chili | 190 cal | 150 cal |

**3. Pile on the toppings:** diced white onion, shredded mild cheddar, sour cream, and
five hot sauces. The one marked HOT means it.

Full ingredients, nutrition and allergens are on
[poster 3](../posters/3%20Chili%20Ingredients%20and%20Nutrition.pdf). Ours is a home
kitchen and isn't allergen-free; if you have a food allergy, ask us and we'll show you the
labels.

## Cooking for 400

- **How much:** 15 gallons in all: 12 of smoky chili (three 4-gallon pots) and 3 of veggie.
  That's about 385 cups at 5 fl oz, since not every trick-or-treater takes food.
- **Friday:** everything slow happens the night before. Pork shoulder and pork belly go on
  the smoker at 275°F for about 3 hours; the beans are boiled hard and simmered; the dried
  ancho and guajillo chiles become a paste with chipotles and garlic; the veggie chili is
  cooked start to finish.
- **Saturday:** only the wet cooking. Each pot gets the aromatics, spices and chile paste,
  then tomatoes, coffee and cocoa, the meat, and finally the beans and the belly bites
  (late, so they stay as bites). Masa harina thickens it and a splash of cider vinegar
  finishes it.
- **Serving:** five slow cookers on high (four smoky, one veggie), split across two
  circuits, held at 135°F or hotter, with toppings kept cold over ice. About 70% of
  visitors come between 4:00 and 6:30, so a refill runner is the most important job.
