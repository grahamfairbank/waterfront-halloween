## Ingredients

Ingredients are listed in order of weight, largest first.

**Smoky Chili:** pinto beans, crushed tomatoes, water (bean cooking liquid), onions, kidney
beans, smoked pork shoulder, diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, ground beef, smoked pork belly,
ground pork, poblano peppers, brewed coffee, green bell peppers, dried ancho and guajillo
chiles, chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, masa harina (corn flour treated with lime), salt,
garlic, cider vinegar, rendered pork and beef fat, brown sugar, chili powder (chili pepper,
spices, salt, garlic powder, tricalcium phosphate), cumin, smoked paprika, unsweetened
cocoa, Mexican oregano, black pepper, cinnamon.

**Mild Veggie Chili (vegan):** black beans, water (bean cooking liquid), pinto beans,
crushed tomatoes, roasted sweet potatoes, onions, diced tomatoes, whole tomatoes, corn,
green bell peppers, poblano peppers, dried ancho and guajillo chiles, vegetable oil, masa
harina (corn flour treated with lime), reduced-sodium vegetable base (vegetables and
vegetable juice concentrate [carrot, celery, onion, tomato], yeast extract, salt, cane
sugar, maltodextrin [corn], canola oil, malted barley extract, onion powder, dried potato,
garlic powder, natural flavor), lime juice, garlic, salt, chili powder (chili pepper,
spices, salt, garlic powder, tricalcium phosphate), smoked paprika, cumin, unsweetened
cocoa, Mexican oregano.

**Corn chips:** corn, vegetable oil, salt.

**Chili mac pasta:** enriched elbow macaroni (durum wheat semolina, enriched with vitamins
and iron), cooked in salted water and tossed with vegetable oil.

**Shredded cheddar:** cheddar cheese (pasteurized milk, cheese culture, salt, enzymes,
annatto color), potato starch to prevent caking, natamycin (a natural mold inhibitor).

**Sour cream:** cultured cream (milk).

**Diced onion:** white onion.

**Hot sauces:** chile peppers, vinegar, water, salt, spices, garlic; some add xanthan gum or
sodium benzoate. Ask to see the bottles.

● contains. The first nine columns are the major food allergens; gluten, corn, pork and beef
are listed for guests who avoid them. Diced onion and the hot sauces contain none of these.

Chili mac (wheat pasta) and the veggie chili (malted barley in the vegetable base) contain
gluten. The smoky chili, chips and toppings are made without gluten ingredients but are not
certified gluten-free. Our kitchen is a home kitchen and is not allergen-free, so
cross-contact is possible. If you have a food allergy, please ask us, and we'll show you the
product labels.
