# The timeline

All the meat is cooked on Friday night, so Saturday is wet cooking and serving only.
([Back to the food page](README.md))

![Chili Night timeline: Wednesday to Saturday](timeline.png)

## Wednesday: shop and thaw

- Move the frozen pork belly and ground pork to the fridge. The belly needs about 2 days.
- Warehouse club: pork shoulder, beef, cheese, sour cream, corn chips, hotel pans, paper goods.
- Mexican grocery or bulk aisle: dried chiles, cumin, oregano, masa harina, dried beans.
- Grocery: tomatoes, produce, hot sauces.

## Thursday evening: about 45 minutes

- Soak the beans in 3 times their volume of water: pinto and kidney together, black beans
  separately.
- Cube the pork shoulder (2-inch) and belly (1-inch), rub them, and refrigerate in separate
  pans overnight.
- Clear fridge space for Friday's beans, the 3 gallons of veggie chili and the cooked meat.
  A cooler packed with ice, at 41°F or below, works for overflow.

## Friday: about 5½ hours

| Time | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 4:30 PM | Light the smoker, 275°F |
| 5:00 PM | Pork shoulder and belly on the smoker. Beans on: the smoky-chili beans in two 20-quart pots on the stove; the black beans and veggie pintos in a third pot on the propane burner. Boil hard 10 minutes, then simmer |
| 5:15 PM | Brown the 10 lb of ground beef and pork in 4 rounds. Drain, save the drippings, spread on sheet pans to cool |
| 6:15 PM | Make the chile pastes (smoky and veggie). Roast the sweet potatoes |
| 6:30 to 7:00 PM | Beans done. Save all the liquid. Portion the smoky-chili beans into 3 containers and cool in shallow pans |
| 7:00 PM | Veggie chili in a freed-up 20-quart pot (about 1½ hours). Meanwhile, chop the aromatics into 3 bags and mix 3 spice bags |
| 8:00 PM | Pork shoulder off at 165°F. Spread on pans to cool |
| 8:30 PM | Belly off when rendered and probe-tender. Veggie chili done. Cool everything in shallow pans |
| 9:00 PM | Weigh each meat, split into thirds, label by pot, refrigerate |
| 9:15 PM | In the garage: set up the table, test every slow cooker, check which outlets are on separate circuits |

## Saturday: Halloween

Smoky chili is done by about 1:15 PM. Serving runs 4:00 to 8:00 PM, with the peak from 4:00
to 6:30.

| Time | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 9:30 AM | All three pots: sweat the aromatics in drippings, bloom the spice bags. Run one pot on the propane burner if the range can't fit three |
| 10:00 AM | Fry the chile paste, then add tomatoes, coffee, cocoa, bean liquid, pork shoulder and ground meat. Bring back to a simmer |
| 10:45 AM | Simmer low for 1 hour |
| 11:45 AM | Beans and belly in. Simmer 1 hour |
| 12:45 to 1:15 PM | Skim, masa, vinegar, salt, taste. Smoky chili done |
| 1:15 to 3:30 PM | Hold: ladle into about 12 lidded foil hotel pans across three ovens at 200 to 225°F. Stir every 30 to 45 minutes and keep it at 135°F or above. About 1½ pans refill one slow cooker |
| 1:30 PM | Dice 6 lb of white onion for the topping. Pan up onion and cheese, fill the sour cream bottles, refrigerate |
| 2:00 PM | Reheat the veggie chili, round 1 (about 1½ gallons), to 165°F |
| 2:30 PM | Garage: stage cups, spoons, chips, napkins, trash, the hot sauce caddy and a light |
| 3:00 PM | Preheat the slow cookers on high with hot water, then dump it before filling |
| 3:30 PM | Fill 5 cookers: 4 smoky, 1 veggie. Toppings out over ice |
| 3:50 PM | Temperature check on every cooker |
| 4:00 to 6:30 PM | Peak, about 280 servings. The table opens with about 190, so expect 3 to 4 refills. Start the veggie round 2 reheat at about 4:30 |
| 6:30 to 8:00 PM | Tapering, about 130 servings and 3 to 4 more refills. Swap in fresh topping pans |
| 8:00 PM | Close. Portion leftovers into shallow containers and refrigerate within 2 hours, or freeze |
