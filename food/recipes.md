# The chili recipes

Both chilis as we make them for about 400 trick-or-treaters: three pots of smoky chili
(4 gallons each) and one pot of veggie chili (3 gallons). Temperatures are in °F.
([Back to the food page](README.md))

## Smoky chili

A familiar beef-and-bean chili, kept mild-medium so the hot sauces can carry the heat. The
twists: smoked pork belly burnt ends that stay as distinct bites, smoked pork shoulder that
melts in, a paste of whole dried chiles, coffee and cocoa for depth, masa harina to thicken,
and a splash of vinegar at the end.

All the meat is cooked on Friday in one go, then weighed and split three ways, so each pot
on Saturday is wet cooking only. The cooked weights below are estimates: weigh what you
actually get and divide by 3.

### Friday: prep for all three pots

| Prep | Raw input | Method | Yield, then per pot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smoked pork shoulder | 12 lb boneless pork butt, 2-inch cubes | Rub; smoke at 275°F about 3 hours, to 165°F inside with good bark | about 8.5 lb, then **about 2.8 lb** |
| Smoked belly burnt ends | 6 to 8 lb pork belly, skin off, 1-inch cubes | Rub; smoke at 275°F about 3 to 3½ hours, until rendered and probe-tender | about 4 lb from 7 lb, then **about 1.3 lb** |
| Browned ground meat | 6 lb ground beef (85/15) + 4 lb ground pork | Brown hard in 4 rounds with about 1 tbsp salt in total; drain | about 7¼ lb (4½ qt), then **about 2.4 lb (1½ qt)** |
| Drippings | from the ground meat and the belly | Save and chill | **¼ cup** |
| Beans | 6¾ lb pinto + 3¾ lb kidney, soaked | Boil hard 10 minutes, then simmer 45 to 75 minutes until just tender; salt in the last 15 | about 17 qt drained, then **about 5½ qt** |
| Bean liquid | from the beans | Save all of it, about 3 gallons | **2½ to 3½ qt**, plus extra |
| Chile paste | 21 ancho + 21 guajillo chiles (toasted and soaked), 9 chipotles + 9 tbsp adobo, 27 garlic cloves, 6 cups bean liquid | Blend in rounds until smooth | about 2¼ qt, then **about 3 cups** |
| Aromatics | 10½ lb yellow onion, 6 green bell peppers, 9 poblanos, 27 garlic cloves | Dice and mince | **3 bags** |
| Spice bags | per pot: 3½ tbsp toasted cumin, 3½ tbsp chili powder, 2½ tbsp Mexican oregano, 2½ tbsp smoked paprika, 1¼ tsp cinnamon | Mix | **3 bags** |

**Pork rub** for all 19 lb of shoulder and belly: ½ cup kosher salt, 3 tbsp black pepper,
3 tbsp brown sugar. Keep the shoulder and the belly in separate pans so they can come off
the smoker at different times.

**Label everything by pot** ("Pot 1: shoulder + ground", "Pot 1: belly", "Pot 1: beans",
"Pot 1: paste" and so on), and Saturday becomes grab-and-dump.

### Saturday: one pot

One 4-gallon batch, about 100 servings, in a 20-quart or larger pot. Make 3.

| Ingredient | Per pot |
| --- | --- |
| Drippings | ¼ cup |
| Aromatics bag | 1 (3½ lb onion, 2 bell peppers, 3 poblanos, 9 garlic cloves) |
| Spice bag | 1 |
| Chile paste | about 3 cups |
| Crushed tomatoes | 3 × 28 oz cans |
| Petite diced tomatoes | 1 × 28 oz can |
| Tomato sauce | 1 × 28 oz can |
| Strong brewed coffee | 1¼ cups |
| Unsweetened cocoa | 2½ tbsp |
| Brown sugar | 2½ tbsp |
| Bean liquid | 2½ qt to start, more to thin |
| Smoked pork shoulder + browned ground meat | about 2.8 lb + about 2.4 lb |
| Cooked beans, drained | about 5½ qt |
| Smoked belly cubes | about 1.3 lb |
| Masa harina + water | ⅔ cup masa whisked into 2 cups water |
| Cider vinegar | 3½ tbsp |
| Kosher salt | 1½ to 3 tbsp, to taste at the end (the meat is already salted) |

1. Heat the drippings. Sweat the aromatics for 10 minutes, then add the spice bag for 1
   minute to bloom.
2. Add the chile paste and fry it for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring.
3. Add the tomatoes, coffee, cocoa, sugar, 2½ qt bean liquid, the pork shoulder and the
   ground meat. The meat is cold, so bring it back to a simmer over medium heat, stirring
   often (30 to 40 minutes). Then simmer low, partly covered, for 1 hour, scraping the
   bottom every 15 minutes.
4. Add the beans and the belly and simmer 1 more hour. The belly goes in late so it stays as
   burnt-end bites. Break up the shoulder with a potato masher.
5. Skim the fat. Stir in the masa slurry and simmer 15 minutes. Finish with the vinegar and
   salt.
6. Keep it a little looser than you would at home, because it thickens while it holds. Thin
   with bean liquid as needed.

## Mild veggie chili

One batch, 3 gallons, about 75 servings. Milder, with no chipotle. The twist is roasted
sweet potato plus smoked paprika, for smokiness without meat. Cook its beans in their own
water and use vegetable bouillon so it stays vegetarian. (As served, with no dairy, it's
also vegan.)

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Dried black beans | 2 lb |
| Dried pinto beans | 1½ lb |
| Sweet potatoes, ½-inch dice, roasted at 425°F for 25 minutes | 3 lb |
| Frozen corn | 1 lb |
| Yellow onions, diced | 2½ lb |
| Green bell peppers | 2 |
| Poblano | 1 |
| Garlic | 6 cloves |
| Crushed tomatoes | 2 × 28 oz cans |
| Petite diced tomatoes | 1 × 28 oz can |
| Whole tomatoes, hand-crushed | 1 × 28 oz can |
| Ancho chiles | 4 |
| Guajillo chiles | 2 |
| Spices | 2 tbsp cumin, 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tbsp Mexican oregano, 2 tbsp smoked paprika |
| Cocoa | 2 tbsp |
| Vegetable bouillon base | 2 tbsp in 2 qt bean liquid or water |
| Masa harina | ⅓ cup in 1 cup water |
| Lime juice or cider vinegar | 2 tbsp |

1. Cook it Friday night in a 20-quart pot, once the beans are out of it. Same method as the
   smoky chili, without the meat steps.
2. Simmer 1 hour after the beans go in. Add the sweet potato and corn for the last 20
   minutes so they hold their shape.
3. Cool in shallow pans and refrigerate.
4. Saturday: reheat in an 8-quart pot in two rounds of about 1½ gallons, bringing each to
   165°F before it goes into a slow cooker.

## The rest of the menu

- **Chili & Chips:** a 1 oz bag of corn chips, crushed, with about 4 fl oz of chili poured in.
- **Chili Mac:** elbow macaroni cooked in salted water and tossed with a little oil, topped
  with chili.
- **Toppings:** diced white onion, shredded mild cheddar, sour cream in squeeze bottles, and
  five hot sauces.
