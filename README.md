# Halloween on Our Street

Every Halloween our family serves trick-or-treaters one **real-food dish** alongside the
candy. It gives people a reason to stop, warm up and talk, and over the years it has
become the thing that brings the neighborhood together in our driveway.

This repository is where we keep the story of the night: the food, the posters, and the
homemade photo booth that a few people asked about. It's documentation only.

## 2026: Chili Night

- **When:** Saturday, October 31, 2026, 4:00 to 8:00 PM, in the driveway
- **Food:** two chilis made from scratch, about 15 gallons for around 400 visitors.
  See [food](food/README.md).
- **Photo booth:** tap the moon, smile for 3·2·1, take your photo home by QR code or email.
  See [photo booth](photo-booth/README.md) for how it works and why it's built the way it is.
- **Share the treats:** three local causes you can give to directly (below, and [its own page](share-the-treats.md)).

## Share the treats

As you enjoy your treats, think of neighbors with fewer. We don't collect anything; every
gift goes straight to the organization.

- [Grafton Food Pantry](https://graftonfoodpantry.org) (Huntley): volunteer-run, serving
  Huntley, Lakewood and parts of Lake in the Hills, Crystal Lake and Algonquin.
- [D300 Food Pantry](https://d300foodpantry.org) (Carpentersville): 100% volunteer-run,
  for District 300 kids, families and school staff.
- [Animal House Shelter](https://animalhouseshelter.com/donate) (Huntley): a no-kill
  shelter that takes in special-needs animals other shelters can't.
