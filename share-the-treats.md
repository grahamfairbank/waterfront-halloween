# Share the Treats

As you enjoy your treats tonight, think of neighbors with fewer. Give to one of these local
causes. This is the text of [poster 4](posters/4%20Share%20the%20Treats.pdf), which has a
QR code for each. ([Back to the night](README.md))

| Cause | Where | What they do | Give |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Grafton Food Pantry** | Huntley | A volunteer-run food pantry serving Huntley, Lakewood and parts of Lake in the Hills, Crystal Lake and Algonquin. | [graftonfoodpantry.org](https://graftonfoodpantry.org) |
| **D300 Food Pantry** | Carpentersville | A 100% volunteer-run pantry for District 300 kids, families and school staff, right here in our district. | [d300foodpantry.org](https://d300foodpantry.org) |
| **Animal House Shelter** | Huntley | A no-kill shelter that takes in special-needs animals other shelters can't, and pays for their care. | [animalhouseshelter.com/donate](https://animalhouseshelter.com/donate) |

We don't collect anything here. Every gift goes straight to the organization.

Happy Halloween!
