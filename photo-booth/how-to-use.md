# Photo Booth: how to use it

*Step into the light... if you dare.* This is the text of
[poster 1](../posters/1%20Photo%20Booth%20How-To.pdf), the one beside the booth.
([Back to the photo booth](README.md))

| | Step | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Tap the moon** | The glowing moon on the screen is the Start button. |
| 2 | **Find your spot** | Stand on the *Stand here* sticker. Bring friends! Keep your group between the *Squeeze in!* stickers. |
| 3 | **Smile for 3 · 2 · 1** | The light comes on for the countdown. Hold still until it clicks off. |
| 4 | **Love it or redo it** | Check your photo, then pick *Retake*, *Email*, *QR code* or *Done*. |
| 5 | **Take it home** | Scan the QR code to save it on your phone, or type your email and we'll send it. |
| 6 | **Missed it?** | Tonight's photos loop on the little gallery screen. Tap it when yours comes around for its QR code. |

**Look closely:** our second camera adds a surprise to every photo. Who else was here?

*Photos are saved. A second camera also photographs the booth area. If you enter an email
address, it is used only to send you your photo.*
